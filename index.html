Most writeups cover finding the issue. This one covers the part that makes the work defensible: a written scope gate, stop-at-proof boundaries, and a record a second person can actually check. Read the whole method free — pay only for the fill-in sheets you use during a test.

What the method gives you

  • Scope gate first — if a target isn't written in scope, it doesn't get tested.
  • Stop at proof — enough to show an issue is real, not a working exploit.
  • A reviewer-ready record — facts, guesses and ruled-out items kept apart, so "we were careful" becomes something you can check.
